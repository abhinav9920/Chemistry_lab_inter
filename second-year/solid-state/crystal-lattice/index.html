How to read a unit cell

1. Look at positionsBlue atoms are shared at corners, orange atoms lie on faces, and pink atoms lie fully inside the cell.
2. Count contributionsA corner contributes ⅛, a face atom contributes ½, and a body atom contributes 1 to one cubic unit cell.
3. Compare dimensionsCubic cells have a = b = c. Tetragonal cells have a = b ≠ c. Hexagonal cells have a = b ≠ c and γ = 120°.
