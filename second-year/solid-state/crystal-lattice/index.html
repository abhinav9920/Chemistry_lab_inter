From unit cell to crystal

1. FrameworkUse edges and small spheres to identify corner, face and body positions. These lines are guides, not bonds.
2. PackingBring equal spheres into contact and connect the touching direction to the radius-edge relation.
3. VoidsHighlight the empty sites between neighbouring particles and count the atoms surrounding each site.
4. LatticeRepeat the unit cell to see how local geometry produces an extended crystal structure.
