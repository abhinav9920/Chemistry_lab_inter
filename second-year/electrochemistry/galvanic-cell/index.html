① AnodeMetal atoms lose electrons and enter the solution as cations. Oxidation occurs here.
② External circuitElectrons travel through the wire from anode to cathode, producing electric current.
③ CathodeMetal ions gain electrons and deposit on the electrode. Reduction occurs here.
④ Salt bridgeAnions migrate toward the anode and cations migrate toward the cathode to maintain electrical neutrality.
