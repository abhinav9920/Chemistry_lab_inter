Do not confuse bonds and forces

Primary bondsIonic, covalent and metallic bonds hold atoms or ions together within a substance.
Hydrogen bondA strong, directional intermolecular attraction; it can also occur within one suitable molecule.
van der Waals forcesDipole–dipole and London forces act between particles and are generally weaker.
Strength mattersStronger attractions usually require more energy to separate particles and raise melting or boiling points.
