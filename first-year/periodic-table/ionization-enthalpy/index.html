Four rules to remember

Successive ionizationIE₂ > IE₁ because a positive ion holds its remaining electrons more strongly.
Atomic radiusLarger distance weakens attraction, so ionization enthalpy decreases.
Effective nuclear chargeGreater net nuclear pull raises the energy required for removal.
Shielding effectMore inner-electron shielding lowers attraction and ionization enthalpy.
