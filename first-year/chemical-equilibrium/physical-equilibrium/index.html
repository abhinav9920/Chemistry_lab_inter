Experiment guide

How to use the Physical Equilibrium Lab

  1. Select one of the three physical equilibria.
  2. Press Start and compare the live forward and reverse rates.
  3. Watch the rate graph and phase-amount graph together.
  4. After equilibrium forms, change the temperature or press Disturb.
  5. Notice that particles still exchange when the amounts appear constant.
