COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

Changes that may not shift equilibrium

A catalyst changes how quickly equilibrium is reached. An inert gas has different effects depending on the condition.

Rule: Adding a reactant shifts equilibrium towards products; removing it shifts equilibrium towards reactants.

Le Chatelier’s principle summary for this model

Concentration

Add reactant → right. Add product → left. Removal produces the opposite response.

Pressure/volume

Compression → fewer gas particles (right). Expansion → more gas particles (left).

Temperature

Heating an exothermic equilibrium → left. Cooling → right.

Catalyst

Both reaction rates increase equally. Equilibrium position and composition do not change.

Investigation guide

Follow the disturbance in three stages

  1. Select a factor tab.
  2. Apply one change and watch the large shift arrow.
  3. Compare the forward and reverse rate bars immediately after the change.
  4. Follow the composition marker and graph until a new equilibrium is established.
  5. Reset before comparing a different factor.
