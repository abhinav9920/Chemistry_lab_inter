Sign convention used here: positive work means work is done on the system, so the piston moves downward and the gas is compressed. Negative work means work is done by the system, so the piston moves upward and the gas expands.

All gas particles are constrained to remain inside the cylinder and below the piston. When the piston moves, the particles remain within the changing gas volume.
