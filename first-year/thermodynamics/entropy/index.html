Microscopic interpretation

S = kB ln Ω

After expansion, each particle has twice the accessible volume.

The number of accessible arrangements increases by a factor of 2N. The evenly spread macrostate is therefore far more probable than every particle remaining on one side.

Ω denotes the number of accessible microstates. Particle counts are a simplified conceptual model; the thermodynamic calculation uses one mole of ideal gas.

Thermodynamic calculation

ΔS = nR ln(V₂/V₁)

ΔS = (1.00 mol)(8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹) ln 2 = +5.76 J K⁻¹

Expansion into a larger accessible volume increases the entropy of the gas.

Key idea

Entropy is not merely a visual measure of “disorder.” It measures how many microscopic arrangements are compatible with the observed macroscopic state. Spontaneous change overwhelmingly favours states with greater statistical weight.
